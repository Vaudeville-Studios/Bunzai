# Shared paper art and hard-cut motion revision

Starting props stay detailed and recognizable. Every prop uses the same Bunzai Persona/Danganronpa-inspired layered-paper illustration style rather than a franchise's native rendering. Existing bunny logo is reused unchanged for YOURS.

Each prop reveals in color, then instantly becomes exactly two flat colors: the phrase's main color and a shadow emphasis color. Broad shadow areas replace edge tracing; source alpha remains transparent. Text is never masked. Thirty-two phrase-specific abrupt transitions replace the shared bounce.

A directional burst of straight paper shards flies out from behind the prop at the cut and clears before the next prop appears. The accelerated ending skips the color hold and lands directly on the two-color cut; longer opening phrases retain the brief full-color reveal. No photographic injury imagery. Reuse bounded geometry and one canvas; honor reduced motion and hidden-tab pause.

## First style-master edit prompt

```text
EDIT TARGET: the isolated ruby slippers in the first reference. Preserve their recognizable silhouette, pair composition, bows, folds and important decorative structure; do not simplify them into an icon. Restyle the ENTIRE object in Bunzai's ONE shared theatrical paper-collage art direction inspired by Persona and Danganronpa. It must look like a detailed illustration physically assembled from razor-cut and hand-torn sheets of colored paper, angular asymmetrical shapes, layered edges and bold flat shadow masses. Keep ruby-red, charcoal and warm ivory, a few deep burgundy shadow layers; draw the sparkling material as deliberate geometric paper flecks, NOT dense stipple/grain or a photorealistic texture. Rich selected interior detail in the SAME collage language. Crisp graphic cut-paper planes, a little handmade edge irregularity, subtle tactile paper fiber only within solid shapes. No airbrush, gradients, 3D gloss, realistic metallic reflection, Victorian ornamental engraving, franchise-native rendering, faux-vector outline icon or thick continuous white sticker border. No background/scenery/frame/caption. Centered isolated complete prop with 10% transparent padding on all sides. True transparent alpha background. This is the style master for a matching 31-object set; the look should be consistent regardless of each object's franchise.
```

All subsequent edits use the original prop as the identity/composition reference and this approved master as the shared style reference. Delivery assets are `<slug>-v2.webp`; original generated PNGs remain retained. Exact edit prompts and generation source paths are recorded in the v2 manifest.
